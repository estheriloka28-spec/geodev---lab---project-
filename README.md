# My GeoDev Lab Africa Project
- Built over twelve months with GeoDev Lab Africa, cohort one.

## Research Question: where are waste dumpsites concentrated in Oshodi-Isolo, and what environmental/health risks does the evidence show are associated with them?

- Month 1 Answer: 6 out of 10 informal dumpsites mapped are within 500m of canals, concentrated in Ago Palace, Bucknor, and Ejigbo. This shows the main risk is drainage blockage and flooding during rainy season.
