# MY PROJECT BRIEF

## The Question
Waste Management in Oshodi-Isolo Local Government Area (LGA): where are waste dumpsites concentrated in Oshodi-Isolo, and what environmental/health risks does the evidence show are associated with them?

## Why It Matters
A gap I have identified in proper waste management in Nigeria is the gap between waste generated and waste properly collected. This gap has allowed dumpsites, drainage blockages, and informal disposal to fill the space left behind.
Similarly, water — an essential resource for human survival — is scarcely monitored. Recent reports have identified polluted drinking water being sold and consumed across Lagos State. But while attention focuses on the organizations producing poorly treated water for consumption, a further question arises: what if the gap in waste management is itself contributing to the poor condition of groundwater and boreholes — the very sources from which drinking water is prepared? In other words, it comes back full-circle. I will focus specifically on one LGA for this project, then scale

## The Data I Need
- Dumpsite locations in Lagos State
- Oshodi-Isolo LGA boundary polygon
- Waterways in Oshodi-Isolo (used as an indicator of shallow water table/groundwater vulnerability, not a direct claim that surface water contaminates boreholes)
- Health/environmental evidence at Oshodi-Isolo
- Health/environmental evidence at neighbouring LGA (Alimosho, borders Oshodi-Isolo)
- LAWMA operational activity (not structured data)

An automated, updatable map for Oshodi-Isolo that:
‎- Filters and displays dumpsite locations within Oshodi-Isolo’s LGA boundary.
‎- Overlays known environmental/health risk points.
